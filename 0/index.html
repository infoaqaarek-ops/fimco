Company Snapshot

Four decades on the factory floor

A founder-built manufacturer that grew alongside Egypt's automotive supply chain — from a single sheet-metal workshop into a certified, multi-plant OEM feeder.

Company Overview

  • Location: 6th of October City, 4th Industrial Zone, Egypt
  • Total area: 12,000 m² (6,800 m² utilized)
  • Core processes: Stamping, Welding, Assembling, Plastic Painting (post-injection)
Certifications
IATF 16949 ISO 9001 ISO 14001 ISO 45001 BIQS
Milestones
1987

Founded as part of MB Group, specializing in sheet metal forming.

Initially located on the Cairo–Alexandria Agricultural Road.

1998

Relocated to 6th of October City, close to car manufacturers.

Introduced a new sector: painting exterior plastic parts.

2020

Expanded production capacity to meet rising market demand.

Implemented advanced manufacturing technologies for higher efficiency.

2025

Focus on global expansion and export growth.

Diversification into multiple sectors within the automotive feeding industry.
